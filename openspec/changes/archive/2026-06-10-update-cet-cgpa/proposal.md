## Why

The user's CGPA at College of Engineering Thiruvananthapuram (CET) has updated to 9.79. To ensure the resume and portfolio website reflect current and accurate academic information, the CGPA needs to be updated in both places.

## What Changes

- Update the CET B.Tech CSE CGPA from 9.42 to 9.79 in `index.html` (the interactive education details modal).
- Update the CET B.Tech CSE CGPA from 9.42 to 9.79 in `resume.html` (the standalone digital resume page).
